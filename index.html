Our Story

Where Every Cup
Tells a Story

Urban Brew Café was born from a simple conviction: that a great cup of coffee deserves an equally great setting. Founded in 2018 by two lifelong coffee obsessives, we curate single-origin beans from small farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala — roasted in-house every Thursday.

Our kitchen works with seasonal, locally sourced produce to craft a menu that complements rather than competes with the coffee. From the morning's first cappuccino to an afternoon slice of cheesecake, every detail is considered.

Come as you are. Stay as long as you like.
